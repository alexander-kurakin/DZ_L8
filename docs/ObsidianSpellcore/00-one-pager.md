## 4. Rules

**Grid:** 4 belts × 16 sectors (Spawn / Outer / Middle / Inner). 
Path N activates index N on all belts. Details: [[world/spellcore]]

**Plants:** 
Toxic O/M 
Turret+Mine O/M/I 

Turret hits N±1 same belt 
Mine = Hit every enemy exactly once after a short delay
Toxic = DoT

**Enemies:** 
Cat (target=Inner, emote + explosion)   
coutered by Mine,LMB 
immune to toxic

Tank (target=Middle, shooting from there) 
countered by Turret,Mine 
immune to toxic, turret only 50% effective vs tank

Dragon (target = Inner, emote+continious DoT)
countered by LMB, Turret, Mine
flies over toxic areas, heals by mine damage by 25%

**LMB:** low effectiveness ability, ~10s CD

**Essence resource:** corpse drops loot→ hover over loot→ loot flies to tower -> tower eats essence
